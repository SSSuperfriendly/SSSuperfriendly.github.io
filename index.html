Hi there! I am an undergraduate student majoring in CS (Elite class) at Guohao College, Tongji University. My research interests include High-Performance AI and AI Systems. I am fortunate to work with Prof. Minghui Liwang at the Shanghai Research Institute for Intelligent Autonomous Systems.

Beyond algorithm research, I am passionate about AI4S and AI for Sustainability. Recently, as the only undergraduate student, I am engaging in cross-modal multi-agent research as part of the 2025 Scientific Intelligence "Hundred Groups, Hundred Projects" Special Program organized by the SMCEI. Prior to this, together with my team, I won the Grand Prize at the 20th National Environmental Friendly Science and Technology Competition.

If you are interested in High-Performance AI or AI for Sustainability, feel free to reach out to me — or book a coffee chat or meeting.
